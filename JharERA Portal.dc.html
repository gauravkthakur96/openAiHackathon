Know your rights in plain wordsसरल शब्दों में आपके अधिकार

Before booking
An unregistered project cannot be sold to you

No promoter may advertise, market, book or sell any plot or apartment without registering the project with the Authority. If there is no registration number on the brochure, walk away.

Section 3, RERA Act 2016
While building
70% of your money must stay in a separate account

Money collected from buyers goes into a dedicated bank account and can only be withdrawn for that project's construction, certified by an engineer and a chartered accountant.

Section 4(2)(l)(D)
On delay
Late possession earns you interest, or your money back

If the promoter misses the possession date in your agreement, you may withdraw with interest and compensation, or stay on and claim interest for every month of delay.

Section 18
